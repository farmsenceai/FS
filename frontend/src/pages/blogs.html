How AI is transforming poultry farming worldwide

FarmSence • Feb 2026

Artificial intelligence is no longer a distant promise for agriculture — it is actively reshaping how poultry farms operate, from the moment a chick hatches to the day a flock reaches market weight. Farms that once relied on daily walk-throughs and manual record-keeping are now using computer vision, environmental sensors, and machine learning models to monitor thousands of birds around the clock, flagging health anomalies, adjusting ventilation automatically, and predicting feed requirements with accuracy that was impossible just five years ago.

Across farms of different sizes, AI is helping teams move from delayed observation to timely action — combining camera signals, environmental data, and flock behaviour into a single, readable picture of daily operations. Early disease detection alone can reduce flock losses by up to 30%, while smart feeding systems cut feed waste significantly by calibrating portions to real-time consumption patterns. The technology is no longer the preserve of large industrial operations; smaller and mid-scale farms are adopting modular AI tools that scale to their specific needs without requiring full infrastructure overhauls.

The broader impact is felt in sustainability too. Optimised ventilation and heating, guided by AI, reduces energy consumption considerably. Water usage monitoring systems have shown reductions in waste without affecting flock welfare. As climate pressures mount and margins tighten, the farms that invest in intelligent infrastructure today are building resilience that will matter for decades. Learn More...
