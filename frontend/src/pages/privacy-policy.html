Privacy Policy

How FarmSence protects the information behind smarter farms

This policy explains how CHICKCOOP FARM PVT. LTD. and FarmSence.ai collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website, book a demo, create an account, publish content, or use our farm intelligence services.

1. Scope and who we are

FarmSence builds AI-enabled poultry and livestock intelligence tools for monitoring, analytics, early disease detection, productivity insights, and farm operations. This policy applies to information collected through our public website, demo and contact forms, account and dashboard experiences, blog features, notifications, and connected services.

If a customer agreement, pilot agreement, or data processing agreement applies to your use of FarmSence services, that agreement may contain additional terms for customer-controlled farm data.

We aim to collect only the information needed to operate our services, support customers, improve farm intelligence workflows, and keep the platform secure.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Contact details such as name, email address, phone number, organisation, role, and location when you contact sales or book a demo.
  • Account details such as login credentials, user role, dashboard preferences, profile details, and authentication activity.
  • Blog and community content such as titles, cover images, article content, review comments, author attribution, publication status, and moderation history.
  • Support or business communications you send to us, including questions, feedback, requests, and consent records.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data such as browser type, IP address, pages visited, referring pages, approximate location, timestamps, and interaction events.
  • Cookie and similar technology data used to keep the site functional, remember preferences, measure performance, and understand marketing effectiveness.
  • Security logs used to detect abuse, prevent unauthorised access, troubleshoot errors, and protect accounts.

3. How we use information

We use information for legitimate business, product, security, and customer support purposes, including to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the FarmSence website, dashboards, account features, blog tools, and AI farm intelligence services.
  • Respond to demo requests, contact form submissions, support questions, and business enquiries.
  • Authenticate users, route users to the correct dashboard, manage roles, and send account or workflow notifications.
  • Moderate blog submissions, maintain review history, publish approved posts, and prevent misuse of public content features.
  • Analyse website and product performance so we can improve reliability, usability, and farm decision workflows.
  • Comply with legal obligations, enforce agreements, protect rights, and maintain platform security.

4. Farm, sensor, and analytics data

FarmSence services may process farm operational information such as flock conditions, behavioural signals, environmental readings, disease indicators, equipment status, productivity insights, and related analytics. This data is used to provide monitoring, alerting, reporting, and intelligence features.

We do not sell farm operational data. Where we use aggregated or de-identified insights to improve models, benchmarks, or product quality, we take reasonable steps to avoid identifying a specific farm, customer, or individual unless authorised.

Customers remain responsible for ensuring they have the necessary rights and notices for information they upload, connect, or submit to FarmSence systems.

5. Sharing and service providers

We may share information with:

  • Trusted service providers that help us host, secure, analyse, communicate, process payments, provide support, or operate the platform.
  • Professional advisers, regulators, law enforcement, or courts where required by law or necessary to protect rights and safety.
  • Business partners or integration providers when you request or authorise an integration, pilot, deployment, or joint service.
  • Successors in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, or sale of business assets, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We require service providers to handle information only for authorised purposes and with appropriate confidentiality and security protections.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and relationship with FarmSence, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of your personal information. You may also request that we update account details, unsubscribe from marketing communications, or review cookie preferences.

For cookie-specific information, please read our Cookies Policy. Essential cookies may be required for site and account functionality.

We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests. Some information may be retained where necessary for legal, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate business recordkeeping purposes.

7. Security, retention, and international processing

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, and disclosure. No digital service can guarantee absolute security, but we work to keep protections appropriate to the nature of the information we process.

We retain information for as long as needed to provide services, maintain accounts, support business operations, comply with law, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods may vary by data type, customer agreement, and legal requirement.

FarmSence may process information in India and other countries where we, our service providers, or infrastructure partners operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border processing.

8. Children, updates, and third-party links

FarmSence services are intended for business and professional use, not for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Our website may link to third-party websites, social platforms, or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this policy.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to make a privacy request, contact FarmSence through our Contact Sales page.
